# Dataset Overview

## 1. Dataset Name
    Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset by Olist

## 2. Dataset Source
    This project uses the Brazilian E-commerce Public Dataset provided by Olist. The dataset contains real e-commerce transactions from a Brazilian online marketplace and is widely used for data analytics, business intelligence, and data science projects.

## 3. Dataset Description
    The dataset contains information related to customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, customer reviews, and geographical locations. It represents the complete order lifecycle, from the moment a customer places an order until the product is delivered and reviewed.

    The data is organized into multiple related tables, allowing analysts to perform relational analysis using SQL and gain business insights from different aspects of the e-commerce process.

## 4. Why This Dataset Was Selected
    This dataset was selected because it closely represents a real-world e-commerce business. It supports end-to-end data analytics by providing information about customer behavior, sales, products, payments, deliveries, and customer satisfaction.

    It is well suited for analyzing customer retention, purchasing behavior, product performance, operational efficiency, and business growth.

## 5. Dataset Structure
    The dataset consists of 9 related tables, each representing a different business entity.

    The tables include:

    Customers
    Orders
    Order Items
    Order Payments
    Order Reviews
    Products
    Sellers
    Geolocation
    Product Category Translation

    These tables are connected through primary and foreign keys, making the dataset suitable for relational database analysis.

## 6. Key Business Entities
    The major business entities represented in the dataset are:

    Customers
    Orders
    Products
    Sellers
    Payments
    Reviews
    Locations

    Each entity contributes to understanding different aspects of the business and customer journey.

## 7. Business Problems That Can Be Solved
    This dataset can be used to answer business questions such as:

    Why are customers not making repeat purchases?
    Which customer segments generate the highest revenue?
    Which product categories perform the best?
    How do payment methods influence purchasing behavior?
    Do delivery delays affect customer satisfaction?
    Which regions contribute the highest sales?
    What factors influence customer retention and long-term business growth?

## 8. Dataset Limitations
    Although the dataset contains rich transactional data, it does not explicitly identify whether a customer has churned. Customer retention and churn must be defined based on business rules and purchasing behavior observed in the data. Additionally, some business information, such as marketing campaigns and customer demographics beyond location, is not available in the dataset.
