# Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

## 1. Customer KPIs
    KPI	                              Description
    Total Custome                     Total number of unique customers who placed orders.
    Repeat Customers 	              Number of customers who placed more than one order.
    Repeat Purchase Rate 	          Percentage of customers who made repeat purchases.
    Customer Retention Rate           Percentage of customers who continued purchasing over time.
    Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) 	  Total revenue generated by a customer during the analysis period.

## 2. Sales KPIs
    KPI	                                 Description
    Total Revenue	                      Total revenue generated from completed orders.
    Total Orders	                      Total number of completed orders.
    Average Order Value (AOV)	          Average amount spent per order.
    Revenue by Product Category	          Revenue generated by each product category.
    Revenue by Region	                  Revenue generated across different customer locations.

## 3. Product KPIs
    KPI	                                  Description
    Best-Selling Product Categories	       Product categories with the highest sales volume.
    Top Revenue-Generating Products	       Products contributing the highest revenue.
    Repeat Purchase Products	           Products frequently purchased by returning customers.

## 4. Operational KPIs
    KPI	                                   Description
    Order Delivery Time	                   Average time taken to deliver orders.
    On-Time Delivery Rate	               Percentage of orders delivered on or before the estimated delivery date.
    Delivery Delay Rate	                   Percentage of orders delivered later than expected.

## 5. Customer Experience KPIs
    KPI	                                   Description
    Average Review Score	               Average customer rating provided after delivery.
    Positive Review Rate	               Percentage of orders with high customer ratings.
    Negative Review Rate	               Percentage of orders with low customer ratings.
    Customer Satisfaction Trend	           Changes in customer satisfaction over time based on review scores.
