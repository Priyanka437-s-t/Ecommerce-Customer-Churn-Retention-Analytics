# Business Questions

## 1. Customer Analysis

1. How many unique customers have placed orders?
2. How many customers made repeat purchases?
3. Which customers generate the highest revenue?
4. Which customer segments contribute the most to overall sales?
5. Which customers appear to be at risk of not making another purchase?

---

## 2. Sales Analysis

6. What is the total revenue generated during the analysis period?
7. What is the average order value?
8. How do sales vary over time (monthly or yearly)?
9. Which regions generate the highest revenue?
10. Which payment methods are most frequently used?

---

## 3. Product Analysis

11. Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
12. Which product categories receive the most orders?
13. Which products encourage repeat purchases?
14. Which product categories perform poorly and may need improvement?

---

## 4. Customer Experience Analysis

15. What is the average customer review score?
16. Do delivery delays affect customer review ratings?
17. Which regions have the highest and lowest customer satisfaction?
18. What are the common characteristics of orders with low review scores?

---

## 5. Operational Analysis

19. What is the average delivery time?
20. Which regions experience the most delivery delays?
21. Are delayed deliveries associated with lower customer satisfaction?

---

## 6. Business Recommendations

22. Which customer segments should be targeted for retention campaigns?
23. Which products should be promoted to increase repeat purchases?
24. What actions can improve customer retention and long-term business growth?
