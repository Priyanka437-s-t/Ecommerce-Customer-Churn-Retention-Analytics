# Business Objectives

## 1. Primary Objective
    The primary objective of this project is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and identify factors that influence customer retention. The goal is to provide data-driven insights that help the company increase repeat purchases, improve customer loyalty, and achieve sustainable business growth.

## 2. Customer Retention Objectives
    Identify customer segments based on purchasing behavior and value.
    Understand why customers do not return after their first purchase.
    Identify high-value customers and customers at risk of becoming inactive.
    Improve customer retention strategies through data-driven insights.
    Increase repeat purchase frequency among existing customers.

## 3. Revenue Growth Objectives
    Identify factors that contribute to revenue growth.
    Analyze customer spending patterns and purchasing trends.
    Understand which customer groups generate the highest revenue.
    Improve customer lifetime value by increasing long-term customer engagement.


## 4. Customer Experience Objectives
    Analyze customer reviews and satisfaction patterns.
    Identify factors that affect customer satisfaction.
    Understand whether delivery performance influences customer experience.

## 5. Product and Sales Objectives
    Identify best-performing product categories.
    Analyze products that encourage repeat purchases.
    Understand regional sales performance.
    Support better product and sales strategies through analytical insights.
