# Stakeholders

## 1. Executive Management
    Executive management is responsible for the overall growth and profitability of the company. They use data to monitor business performance, evaluate strategic initiatives, and make long-term business decisions.

    Key Questions
    Is customer retention improving?
    What are the major factors affecting business growth?
    Which areas require immediate attention?
    How can revenue and profitability be increased?

## 2. Marketing Team
    The marketing team is responsible for attracting new customers and retaining existing ones. They use customer insights to design marketing campaigns and promotional strategies.

    Key Questions
    Which customers are most likely to make repeat purchases?
    Which customer segments require retention campaigns?
    Which products should be promoted?
    How can customer loyalty be improved?

## 3. Sales Team
    The sales team focuses on improving product sales and increasing revenue. They analyze customer purchasing behavior and product performance.

    Key Questions
    Which product categories generate the highest sales?
    Which products encourage repeat purchases?
    Who are the highest-value customers?
    Which regions generate the most revenue?
4.   Operations Team

## 4. Operations Team
    The operations team manages order processing, shipping, and delivery. They monitor operational efficiency to ensure a smooth customer experience.

    Key Questions
    Are orders delivered on time?
    Which regions experience delivery delays?
    Do delivery issues affect customer satisfaction?
    How can operational performance be improved?

## 5. Customer Support Team
    The customer support team works to improve customer satisfaction by resolving customer issues and analyzing feedback.

    Key Questions
    What are the common reasons for negative reviews?
    Which customers are dissatisfied?
    What factors influence customer satisfaction?
    How can the overall customer experience be improved?


## 6. Sellers
    Sellers use the platform to reach customers and increase sales. They are interested in understanding product performance and customer demand.

    Key Questions
    Which products sell the most?
    Which product categories perform best?
    How can repeat purchases be increased?
    Which regions have the highest customer demand?
