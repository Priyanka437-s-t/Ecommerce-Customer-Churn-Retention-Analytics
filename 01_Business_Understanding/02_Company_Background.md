# Company Background

## 1. Company Overview
    This project is based on an e-commerce marketplace that connects customers with sellers through an online platform. Customers can browse products, place orders, make payments, and receive deliveries, while sellers use the platform to list and sell their products. The company operates as an intermediary, providing a seamless shopping experience and supporting transactions between buyers and sellers.

## 2. Industry
    The company operates in the E-commerce and Online Retail Marketplace industry. This industry enables customers to purchase products online from multiple sellers through a single digital platform. It focuses on convenience, product variety, secure payments, and efficient order fulfillment.

## 3. Business Model
    The company's business model is based on connecting customers and sellers through an online marketplace. Sellers register on the platform and list their products for sale. Customers browse available products, place orders, complete payments, and receive their purchases through the delivery network. After receiving their orders, customers can provide ratings and reviews based on their shopping experience. The company manages the platform, facilitates transactions, and ensures a smooth buying and selling process.


## 4. Products and Services
    The platform offers a wide range of products across multiple categories, allowing customers to purchase items from different sellers in one place. In addition to product sales, the platform supports services such as online payment processing, order management, delivery coordination, and customer review collection to improve the overall shopping experience.

## 5. Customers
    The company's primary customers are individual consumers who shop online for a variety of products. These customers come from different regions and have different purchasing behaviors, preferences, and spending patterns. Understanding customer behavior is essential for improving customer satisfaction, increasing repeat purchases, and building long-term customer relationships.   

## 6. Revenue Model
    The company generates revenue by facilitating transactions between customers and sellers. Its revenue primarily comes from marketplace commissions and service fees associated with completed orders. As the number of successful transactions and repeat customers increases, the company's overall revenue and long-term profitability also improve.


## 7. Business Goals
    The company's key business goals are:

    Increase overall sales and revenue.
    Improve customer retention and encourage repeat purchases.
    Enhance customer satisfaction through better shopping experiences.
    Support sellers in achieving higher sales performance.
    Strengthen long-term business growth by building customer loyalty and improving operational efficiency.
