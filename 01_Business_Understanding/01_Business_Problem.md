# Business Problem

## 1. Current Business Situation
    The e-commerce marketplace has experienced steady growth in customers, orders, and product offerings. However, management has observed that many customers purchase only once and do not return to make additional purchases. While attracting new customers continues to generate sales, the company is concerned about maintaining long-term customer relationships and improving repeat purchase behavior.

## 2. Problem Statement
    Customer retention has become a key business challenge. A significant number of customers are not making repeat purchases after their first order. Management wants to understand the factors influencing customer purchasing behavior and identify opportunities to improve customer retention, increase repeat purchases, and support sustainable business growth.

## 3. Business Impact
    Low customer retention can negatively affect business performance in several ways. It increases the need to acquire new customers, which often requires additional marketing investment. Fewer repeat purchases may reduce long-term revenue, lower customer lifetime value, and limit opportunities to build loyal customer relationships.


## 4. Project Objective
    The objective of this project is to analyze customer, order, product, payment, and review data to understand customer purchasing patterns and identify factors that influence customer retention. The analysis aims to provide data-driven insights and recommendations that help management improve customer retention and overall business performance.


## 5. Scope of Analysis
    This project will analyze customer demographics, order history, product categories, payment methods, delivery performance, and customer reviews. The analysis will focus on identifying purchasing trends, repeat purchase behavior, high-value customers, product performance, and other business factors that influence customer retention.


## 6. Expected Business Outcome
    The project is expected to help management better understand customer behavior, identify opportunities to improve customer retention, support data-driven decision-making, and develop strategies that encourage repeat purchases, improve customer satisfaction, and drive sustainable business growth.
